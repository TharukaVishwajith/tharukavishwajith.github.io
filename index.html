    01About

    From a vague problem to a working solution.

    The work I enjoy most starts with a fuzzy requirement: a team knows something is broken or possible, but not what to build. I sit with the people who have the problem, work out what they actually need, propose a solution across software, data and AI, and then build and deploy it with them.

    That's the forward-deployed way of working, and my background covers the whole stack it needs. I trained as a software engineer, did my master's in big data, and most recently architected and led agentic AI and RAG platforms at Rozie AI, from data ingestion to production LLM systems for enterprise customers.

    Years of lecturing, mentoring and teaching on Wizard LK mean I can explain a design to an engineer, a manager or a customer in their own terms, which is also why I'm building {{ site.learn.name }}.
