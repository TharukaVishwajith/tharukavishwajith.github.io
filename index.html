    01About

    Engineer, teacher, builder.

    I started out writing Java and Spring for enterprise and industrial-automation systems, moved through EdTech and data at Pearson, and spent two years lecturing software engineering, data science and ML at the University of Kelaniya.

    Most recently, as a Senior Software Engineer in Rozie AI's Applied AI & ML team, I architected and led agentic AI and RAG platforms: systems that ingest data from anywhere, let people ask questions in plain language and answer with grounded text and charts.

    I care about the whole path from raw data to a useful answer, and about explaining it well, which is why I'm building {{ site.learn.name }}.

    Platform & backend

    Python and Java services, APIs and search infrastructure that hold up under real enterprise load.
